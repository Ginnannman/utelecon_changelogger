  ### About Sharing Files
- In Google Drive, it is possible to upload files of any format such as documents, presentations, music, photos, and videos. However, it is recommended to use Google Spreadsheet, Google Docs, or OneDrive because editing Excel or Word files uploaded on the drive may cause problems such as garbled characters.
