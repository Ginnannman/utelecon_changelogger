+ # Using Paid Google Services
+ Google Payments is disabled for ECCS Cloud Email accounts.
+ Paid services that require payment through Google Payments, such as Google Colab Pro and YouTube Premium, cannot be used with an ECCS Cloud Email account.
+ If you need to use such services, please use a Google Account other than your ECCS Cloud Email account (e.g., XXX@gmail.com).
+ In addition, as a general rule, the use of paid features of Google Cloud Platform with an ECCS Cloud Email account is prohibited.
